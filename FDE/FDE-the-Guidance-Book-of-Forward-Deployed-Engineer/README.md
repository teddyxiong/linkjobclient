## About the Book

In the summer of 2025, one number flooded the author's social feed: **95%**.

*The GenAI Divide*, a 2025 report from MIT's NANDA Lab, found that over the previous three years companies worldwide had burned
$30–40 billion on generative AI — and 95% of those projects produced no value that could be written into a financial statement.
Almost simultaneously, another signal ran the opposite way: on Silicon Valley job boards, postings for a role called
**Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)** grew more than sevenfold in a single year. OpenAI was hiring, Anthropic was hiring, and
over a hundred YC-backed startups were hiring.

Put the two headlines side by side and the answer is not hard to guess: **models are no longer scarce — the scarce thing is
people who can shove a model into a customer's real business.**

The book answers three questions:

- **What an FDE is** — how this role, grown out of Palantir's intelligence projects, is defined and why it exploded in the AI era
- **How it is done** — following one real delivery end to end: finding the right problem, winning the customer, activating the
  deployment, holding the renewal, expanding revenue, and scaling what works
- **Who is doing it** — 165 verifiable cases, from Palantir, OpenAI, Anthropic, Harvey and Sierra to China's first practitioners

Every figure and case in the book is sourced in Appendix C.
