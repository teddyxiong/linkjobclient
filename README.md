## What Is Linkjob AI?

Linkjob AI is a desktop **AI interview assistant** for live interviews, coding assessments, and interview practice. It delivers real-time answers through an invisible overlay that works with any meeting platform or browser-based test — or straight to your phone with **Dual-Device Mode**.

Whether you are explaining a past project, working through an algorithm, or answering a system design question, Linkjob AI helps you organize your thoughts and communicate your approach clearly and confidently.

Available for **Windows, macOS, and Linux**.

## ✨ Why Linkjob AI

### 100% Invisible and Undetectable

The AI Interview Copilot operates in complete stealth across all platforms, running quietly in the background even during screen sharing. With no visible interface or pop-ups, you get real-time AI support without worrying about eye-tracking or proctoring detection. Stay focused and confident while the interview remains completely natural.

### Dual-Device Mode — Answers on Your Phone

**New.** Send AI answers to your phone while the desktop app keeps running in the background, so you can avoid detection by desktop monitoring software. The same live assistance, delivered on a second screen.

### Works on Any Platform

Linkjob AI is invisible on all meeting and assessment platforms. Only the **Zoom desktop app** requires a one-time setting to be enabled beforehand — no additional setup is needed anywhere else.

### Answers That Sound Like You

Add your resume and job details to get answers tailored to your experience, skills, and target role. Linkjob AI draws on your background to suggest relevant examples and natural wording, so you stay specific and consistent through follow-up questions.

### Stay Focused on the Conversation

Keep useful guidance within easy reach without breaking eye contact or losing the thread. Fine-tune your AI so responses stay on topic, substantive, and relevant.

## 🚀 Core Capabilities

### Real-Time Interview Guidance

Linkjob AI understands interview questions as they happen and provides clear, structured guidance to help you organize your response and keep up with follow-ups.

Explore the [real-time AI interview copilot](https://www.linkjob.ai/interview-copilot/).

### Coding Interview Copilot

Get real-time help with coding questions, algorithms, complexity and edge cases, debugging, code optimization, and system design trade-offs. The copilot stays invisible to:

- **Screen sharing** — no visible window or overlay during shared sessions;
- **Active tab detection** — a native desktop app running outside the browser sandbox;
- **Cursor tracking** — invisible cursor and undetectable global hotkeys;
- **Webcam monitoring** — a transparent floating window you can drag over your code editor.

It also supports **live screen capture** (full-screen and partial), multiple screenshots in one conversation, and context-aware answers to interviewer follow-up questions.

Explore the [AI coding interview assistant](https://www.linkjob.ai/coding-interview-copilot/).

### Browser Test & Online Quiz Assistance

Use Linkjob AI during browser-based assessments and online quizzes for instant problem analysis and structured solutions — while staying undetectable on the assessment platform.

### AI Mock Interview Practice

Rehearse behavioral, technical, and coding interviews with AI feedback before the real thing, and refine your answers with your resume and target role in context.

### Smart Screenshot

Capture just the part of the screen that matters and feed it into the conversation for fast, precise context.

Run on top-tier models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and xAI — including GPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok families — with unlimited usage on paid plans. Switch between models to balance response speed and reasoning depth.

## 🚀 Quick Start

1. **Download and install** the version matching your system.
2. **Open Linkjob AI** and sign in, then choose a plan or activate your subscription.
3. **Configure audio and permissions** for the features you will use, including audio input, screen capture, and the Zoom setting if applicable.
4. **Choose an AI model** included in your plan and add your resume, job description, or custom instructions.
5. **Pick your mode** — live interview overlay, Dual-Device Mode, or coding copilot.
6. **Run a practice session** to check audio capture, screenshot assistance, response quality, and screen-sharing behavior.

Before your first real session, go through the [**Required Setup**](./required-setup.md) checklist — Zoom configuration, audio output, VPN, and System Prompt preparation.
