Check these settings before your interview, assessment, or practice session.

      <h3>2. Avoid Changing App Settings During the Session</h3>
      <p>Once your interview or test has started, avoid opening Settings or repeatedly showing/hiding the app.</p>
      <p>Changing app state during an active session may interrupt your workflow or affect how the current testing or meeting environment behaves.</p>
    </td>
  </tr>
</table>

<table>

    <td valign="top">
      <h3>3. Using Headphones? Select Them as Your Audio Output</h3>
      <p>If you're wearing headphones or earphones, make sure your computer's audio output device is set to the headphones you're actually using.</p>
      <p>An incorrect output device may prevent Linkjob AI from hearing the other person's audio correctly.</p>
    </td>
  </tr>
</table>

<table>

    <td valign="top">
      <h3>4. Outside the US or Europe? Connect to a VPN</h3>
      <p>If you're located outside the United States or Europe, connect to a VPN before opening Linkjob AI.</p>
      <p>Make sure your VPN is running in TUN / Virtual Network Adapter mode for the most reliable connection.</p>
    </td>
  </tr>
</table>

    <td valign="top">
      <h3>5. Prepare Your System Prompt in Advance</h3>
      <p>Add your resume, target role, job description, company information, and any other useful context to the System Prompt before the session begins.</p>
      <p>You can also tell the AI what role it should play and how you want answers to be structured.</p>
    </td>
  </tr>
</table>

      <h3>6. If the AI Doesn't Respond as Expected</h3>
      <p>Occasionally, an AI model may misunderstand the context of a practice question or decline to answer.</p>
      <p>For mock interviews, practice sessions, or other permitted uses, you can:</p>
      <ul>
        <li>Add more context in your System Prompt</li>
        <li>Clarify that you're working through a mock or practice scenario</li>
        <li>Ask a follow-up question in the text box</li>
        <li>Try another available AI model</li>
      </ul>
